DATA CLEANING & VISUALIZATION PROJECT

1. Objective
The objective of this project is to clean a raw customer purchase dataset, handle missing values, duplicates and outliers, and visualize important business insights using Python.

2. Dataset
The dataset contains customer demographics, income, purchase amount, product category, city and ratings.

3. Data Cleaning
• Missing numeric values were replaced using the median.
• Duplicate records were identified and removed.
• Extreme values in Age and Purchase_Amount were handled using the IQR method.
• The cleaned dataset was exported as cleaned_customer_purchases.csv.

4. Visualization
The project includes:
• Average purchase amount by product category
• Total purchase amount by city
• Age distribution
• Monthly income versus purchase amount

5. Key Findings
• Electronics has a comparatively high average purchase amount.
• Major cities contribute a large share of total purchases.
• Most customers fall within a normal working-age range after outlier treatment.
• Higher monthly income generally shows a positive relationship with purchase amount, although the relationship is not perfect.

6. Tools Used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

7. Expected Learning Outcome
This project demonstrates the complete basic workflow of data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, visualization and data storytelling.
